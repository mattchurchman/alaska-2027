## Juneau
- Capital Transit (first-party, juneaucapitaltransit.org/glacier): routes 3, 4, 8 stop at Dredge Lake Rd, closest to the visitor center, about 1.5 mi (30 min) away on a flat paved, wheelchair-accessible bike path. Adult fare $2 cash, no change. Up to ~1.5 h each way incl. the walk; bus ride up to 1 h. Frequency not stated.
- CruiseHive port guide (secondary): Franklin Dock and the Cruise Ship Terminal a few minutes' walk to downtown, both fully wheelchair accessible; AJ Dock about 1 mi south of center, $5 shuttle to the tram station/central Juneau; Red Dog Saloon 0.3 mi, Hangar on the Wharf 0.4 mi from the docks.
- USFS Mendenhall map (gov): 1/3 mi paved round trip (~15 min); Nugget Falls 2 mi round trip gravel, moderate; Trail of Time 1 mi loop; Steep Creek 0.25 mi loop (partly closed in salmon season); "fee area", amounts not on the map. The Nugget Falls trail page says 0.8 mi without saying one way or round trip.
- City museum walking tours (juneau.org, 2026): Historic Downtown 90 min $31.50, Tue–Thu 1:45 pm, last tour Jun 30 2026; Geology ~1.5 mi; Capitol tour free weekdays 1:30/3:00 pm, no reservations. Tours may include inclines, stairs, uneven ground.
- GPSmyCity self-guided intro walk: ~1.1 mi, ~2 h, 12 stops, first stop Juneau Cruise Port, narrow streets + wooden boardwalks.
- alaska.org trails: Perseverance ~3 mi moderate from Basin Road; Mount Roberts difficult, steep, trailhead ~1 mi from downtown (Basin Road); easy options: Airport Dike Trail 1 mi paved, Kaxdigoowu Heen Dei 2 mi paved.
- Rental (forum, older-parent thread): Juneau Car Rental and Avis downtown, ~20 min walk from some berths; downtown office reportedly closes ~2 pm on cruise days; airport cheaper but needs cab/bus. No source on ship-day sellouts. juneaucarrental.com is robots-blocked: not read, no rate captured.

## Skagway
- skagway.com (first-party, visitor bureau): SMART bus dock-to-downtown 7 am–9 pm on ship days, $5 all day or $3 each way. On-demand shuttle (24 h notice outside regular hours): dock to River Bridge $5, end of pavement on Dyea/Liarsville Rd $10, Dyea $15. Car rentals listed: Affordable Car Rentals (648 Broadway), Drive About Car Rental (202 9th Ave).
- CruiseHive (secondary): most ships use Broadway Dock; Railroad and Ore docks at the south end of town; under 1 mi / ~18 min walk to downtown; Red Onion ~11 min; helicopter base on the waterfront 5–15 min from the ship; train can pick up at the cruise terminal; all terminals/piers wheelchair accessible.
- NPS short hikes (gov), round trip from the NPS visitor center: Yakutania Point 1–2 mi, 100 ft, easy (footbridge, rock steps); Gold Rush Cemetery + Lower Reid Falls 4 mi, 50 ft, easy; Lower Dewey Lake 0.9 mi to the lake, 3.6 mi around, 500 ft, moderate (wooden staircase, steep uphill).
- Rental + Canada (forum): US citizens can drive a US rental into Canada with passports; Avis cars barred from gravel (Dyea Rd); Sourdough allows Dyea; lines at the counter on ship days; border crossing took minutes. NO first-party statement found from any renter; Avis's Skagway page errored. Wikipedia: US inspection station at Mile 6.8 of the Klondike Hwy; border hours not captured. Kayak shows only Turo-branded Nov dates: not usable as a June 2027 price.
- Not captured: drive times to Carcross/Emerald Lake, dock-by-dock distances, any ship-day sellout data.

## Ketchikan
- Princess blog: four downtown berths (1–4, south to north); a fifth berth at Ward Cove with a shuttle; Creek Street/visitor center short, flat walk (no distance); Saxman ~2 mi south; Totem Bight a few miles north.
- Alaska Cruise Shirts guide (secondary): Creek Street 1–2 h with Dolly's House, boardwalk flat but slippery; Totem Heritage Center ~15 min walk from downtown; downtown has some hills and stairs.
- Princess Historical Ketchikan & Creek Street Walking Tour: ~9 blocks, Easy, wheelchair accessible (scooters/electric chairs OK; manual chair needs companion), 1.5 h; page shows no price.
- FlashpackingAmerica (secondary, May 2023): Silver Line north to Totem Bight, bus enters the park; $2 cash per ride; about hourly (:25 northbound, :13 southbound); ~25 min from the port; Ward Cove stop ~5 min / ~3 mi to Totem Bight; park entry $5 by card at a self-pay station. alaska.org says $1 fare and two free summer dock shuttles (older). Borough transit page lists no fare text (fares page not read).
- Rental (forum): Alaska Car Rental picked up/returned at the dock; poster says Settlers Cove ~20 min drive, Totem Bight ~10 min beyond toward town, Ward Lake 1.3 mi flat loop. Poster's ~$60/day is anecdotal: not stored as a price.
- alaska.org: Deer Mountain Trail 5 mi, ~2,600 ft gain, difficult; trailhead ~0.5 mi from downtown by the directions given.
