# Real-device test (about 10 minutes)

Open https://mattchurchman.github.io/alaska-2027/ in **Safari** on an iPhone, then repeat tasks 1, 4, 6 and 9 on an iPad (try portrait and landscape). Write down anything that looks wrong or fails, with the task number, and send it as a text or screenshot.

1. **Loads.** The page shows Ideas with photos, not "Loading the trip data…". Scrolling sideways should not move the page.
2. **Ideas → Route.** Tap a route card. The map appears with tiles, colored lines and port pins.
3. **Map gestures.** Pinch to zoom and drag the map. The page itself should not zoom or jump. Tap a pin; a popup opens.
4. **Line & ship.** Pick a ship. Photo and facts show. Tap a video thumbnail: it loads and plays.
5. **Dates.** Tap a price dot. It gets a ring, the route highlights, and a panel shows the sailing. Dots should be easy to hit with a thumb.
6. **Days.** Tap Plan the days. Add an Easy-for-parents tour to Parents and an Adventure tour to Brothers. Both appear on that day's time bar. Try a long tour on a short port day: it should be disabled with a reason.
7. **Walking meter.** Add several walking options for parents until the meter passes 2 mi. A warning should appear.
8. **Cost.** Open Cost. Total and the parents/brothers split show. Tap "add my quote" on one price, type a number. The total changes and the line says family quote.
9. **Share.** Tap Share, send the link to yourself by text, and open it. It should land on the same sailing and plan.
10. **Shortlist.** Star two plans. Tap Backup: a file downloads (iOS shows a Downloads prompt). Tap Import and restore it. Turn the phone sideways: nothing should be cut off.

Also notice: any tap target that is hard to hit, text that is too small, a sticky bar covering content, or a screen that scrolls sideways.
